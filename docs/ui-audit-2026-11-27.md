# UI audit, 2026-11-27

Screenshots are in `~/personal_projects/site-photos/matinee-ui/before/`
(outside the repo):
- `phone-*` (390×844) and `desktop-*` (1440×900): full pages.
- `fold-*`: the first phone screen.
- `phone-night-*`: 11:40 PM.

The clock was set to Tuesday Dec 1, 10:30 AM New York, so the board is
busy, and one show (Wicked) is followed. The question for each finding:
would a designer who goes to the theater have made it this way?

## Ranked findings, worst first

### 1. The board's first phone screen is mostly chrome (`/rush`, `fold-rush.png`)

Before the first show, a phone sees:
- a two-line technical caption ("37 productions checked · 33 with programs
  · oldest check November 27 · 17 unverified");
- a link;
- a search field and an unlabeled lightbulb button;
- a row of four icon chips.

The board starts about 37% of the way down, and two shows fit on the
screen. This is the product, opened minutes before a lottery closes.

### 2. The board's rows don't say when to act, in the same words twice (`/rush`, `fold-rush.png`)

- **Lotteries count down:** "3 hr 30 min left". Rush says only "Open now",
  with no "until".
- **Elsewhere it's a clock:** `/today` says "closes 2 PM"; Hamilton says
  "Open til Thu 12 PM"; one row says "Opens 12 PM".
- **What a New Yorker says:** "closes 3", "till 8", "noon", not "12 PM",
  and certainly not a countdown for something closing hours from now.
- **Where to enter** takes a tap to find on every row.

### 3. Empty sections are drawn as boxes (`/rush`, `fold-rush-2.png`)

"LATER TODAY 0 · No more entry windows open later today." and "COMING UP
0 · No upcoming programs found." are each drawn as a white box, even when
there is nothing to say. Two empty containers in a row is the most
template-looking part of the app.

### 4. On the show page, the loudest thing is "Interested" (`/shows/wicked`, `fold-show-wicked.png`)

- The order down the page:
  - a filled crimson-black "Interested" pill (a bookmark);
  - a full-price "Official tickets" card with a green check badge and an
    icon in a tinted circle;
  - a "Follow this show" card with another icon in a circle and an
    explainer line;
  - only then "Ways to save", the reason anyone opened the page.
- Three stacked rounded cards of the same shape and weight: one of the
  listed tells.

### 5. "Ways to save" says everything twice (`fold-show-wicked-2.png`)

- **The paragraph repeats the rows.** A body-size paragraph ("Wicked has a
  $45 rush at the box office and a $55 digital lottery on Broadway Direct.
  Checked November 27…") restates the rows right below it. It's needed for
  search, but it doesn't need to be the loudest text on the page.
- **Each expanded row is dense,** six to eight lines of grey caption, and
  repeats "Checked Nov 27 · wickedthemusical.com · Report a wrong fact".

### 6. Discover's top is the template hero (`/`, `fold-discover.png`)

- The one live fact, "26 shows open now", sits in a pink banner with an
  icon in a white circle and an arrow.
- Below it, a white card holds two more icon-in-circle rows, each with an
  explainer subtitle ("Every Broadway house, one live map", "Day-by-day
  windows for your visit").
- Then a chip row with an icon on every chip.

### 7. A new visitor's profile is a grid of zeros (`/profile`, `fold-profile.png`)

- **The identity card** holds a five-tile stat grid: "0 Shows logged · 0
  Entries · 0 Wins · $0 Money saved · 0 Following". Stat-tile grids were
  already cut from the Record tab as a tell; on a first visit this one
  says nothing.
- **The empty state** is the stock icon plus a cheerful sentence with an
  ellipsis: "Log a show you've seen and your diary starts here…".

### 8. Icons on every chip, badge and row (`/`, `/rush`, show page)

- **Every filter chip carries an icon:** Program, Platform, Circuit and
  Under $50 on `/rush`; Broadway, Rush & lottery and $ on Discover.
- **Badges:** "Official tickets" has a check badge.
- **Cards:** Follow and Official tickets each have an icon in a tinted
  circle.
- **The rule broken:** DESIGN.md §4 allows icons beside a label in primary
  pills, not as decoration on everything.

### 9. `/today` looks ticked before it is (`/today`, `fold-today.png`)

- **Unticked looks done:** an unticked row shows a grey check mark in a
  beige circle, so the list looks complete before anything is entered.
- **Stiff labels:** "Enter on Official site" (capital O).
- **A long intro:** five lines explaining the page to itself.

### 10. Small inconsistencies that add up

- **The same thing named four ways:** "Digital lottery" (board), "Lott…
  $45" (Discover card, truncated), "Lotteries open now" (today), "Ways to
  save" (show).
- **Spaced capitals on nearly every section:** Discover, Rush, Today,
  Show, Profile. DESIGN.md §13 meant the eyebrow as a program-page
  convention, but it has become every section head.
- **"Broadway · Musical" on every board card:** it tells someone choosing
  between lotteries nothing.

## Accents to count

- **Discover's first screen:**
  - the pink banner;
  - three icon circles;
  - a filled crimson-black chip;
  - posters.

  Five competing accents.
- **The board's first screen:**
  - the underlined link;
  - the lightbulb;
  - four icon chips;
  - green dots on every row.

  Nothing is loudest.

## What already works

Keep these:
- The ivory and velvet palette.
- Bodoni titles.
- The poster-first cards.
- Quiet hairline rows with prices in bold.
- The `/today` row ("Digital lottery · $45 · closes 2 PM" and where to
  enter).
- The dot leaders on "The house".
- The desktop two-column board.
